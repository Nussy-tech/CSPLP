Research Information

Research Title: The Impact of the Use of Personalized Analogies in the Learning of Computer Studies in Senior Secondary Schools in Ekiti State.

You are invited to participate in an academic research study conducted as part of a First Degree Project in the Department of Science Education, Ekiti State University.

The purpose of this study is to examine how personalized analogies can influence students' understanding and learning of Computer Studies concepts.

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or withdraw at any stage before submitting your responses.

No information collected will be used for purposes other than academic research. All responses will be treated confidentially and analyzed collectively.

The study involves completing learning activities, short assessments, and a questionnaire. The estimated completion time is approximately 25–35 minutes.
